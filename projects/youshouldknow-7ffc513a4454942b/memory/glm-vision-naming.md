Per official Z.AI docs: **GLM-5-Turbo is a text-only model** (no image/video input). **GLM-5V-Turbo** is the variant that adds image/video input. The "V" suffix denotes Vision. Confirmed in response to the user asking whether GLM-5-Turbo "有眼睛" (has eyes) — it does not; use the V variant for vision. Related to my own lack of native vision: [[vision-ocr-pipeline]].
